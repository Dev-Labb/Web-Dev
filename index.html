Helping Pets and People Find Their Way Home

Welcome to Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Twin Cities Animal Rescue connects animals in need with caring adopters, foster homes, volunteers, and community partners. We believe every pet deserves safety, patience, and the opportunity to thrive.

Our Impact

  • More than 300 animals supported each year.
  • More than 100 volunteer opportunities available throughout the year.
  • Foster homes provide temporary care for pets while they wait for adoption.
  • Community partners help connect pets with food, supplies, and veterinary resources.

How You Can Help

Adopt

Give a pet a permanent home and begin a new chapter together. Learn about our adoption process on the Services page.

Foster

Open your home temporarily to a pet that needs a safe place, daily care, and attention before adoption.

Volunteer

Support pets and people by helping with animal care, events, transportation, outreach, or administrative tasks.
