Ways Twin Cities Animal Rescue Supports Pets

Adoption Process

  1. Review available pets and learn about their individual needs.
  2. Submit an adoption-interest inquiry through our contact page.
  3. Speak with a rescue team member about your home, lifestyle, and pet experience.
  4. Meet the pet and complete the adoption steps if the match is a good fit.

Our goal is to create thoughtful, lasting matches between pets and adopters. We encourage potential adopters to ask questions and take time to find the right companion.

Foster Program

Foster homes provide temporary care for pets that are waiting for adoption, recovering from a transition, or benefiting from more individual attention. Fostering helps us learn about each animal’s personality and needs in a home environment.

Foster Responsibilities May Include

  • Providing food, water, shelter, and daily care.
  • Helping a pet adjust to a safe home environment.
  • Sharing updates about the pet’s behavior and progress.
  • Transporting the pet to approved appointments or adoption events when needed.

Volunteer Opportunities

Animal Care Support

Help with feeding, cleaning, enrichment activities, socialization, and preparing animals for adoption events.

Event and Outreach Support

Help welcome visitors, share information about rescue programs, and support community adoption events.

Transportation and Supply Support

Assist with moving donated supplies or safely transporting animals to approved foster homes, events, or appointments.

Volunteer Connection

Volunteers help create calm, positive experiences for animals while they wait for their next home.
